SLIDE 7: KHAO SAT HE THONG LIEN QUAN
========================================

Cac nen tang hien co:

   khó loc, FOMO, rao can ngon ngu & mui gio.

   CTV thu cong, phan tan tren nhieu ken.

→ Khoang trong thi truong: thieu nen tang tich hop, chuyen biet, ho tro
nguoi dung xuyên suot tu tim kiem → tham du → xac thuc minh chung.

(Ghi nho: phan khao sat chi neu website cua minh noi bat, khong can chi
tiết cac website khac)

========================================
SLIDE 8: CONG NGHE SU DUNG
========================================

Flutter (Frontend - App di dong):
- Cross-platform: 1 ma nguon → chay Android & iOS
- Hien hao 60fps, Hot Reload tang nang suat phat trien
- Render bang Skia, khong phu thuoc platform

Java Spring Boot (Backend):
- Framework Java, "convention over configuration"
- Tu dong cau hinh, giam ma boilerplate
- Tich hop Spring Security & Spring Data JPA
- Dong goi thanh JAR, de trien khai

PostgreSQL (Supabase):
- He quan tri CSDL quan he doi tuong, pho bien nhat 2025
- Dam bao tinh ACID, ho tro full-text search

Firebase Authentication:
- Dich vu BAAS cua Google, ho tro nhieu phuong thuc dang nhap
- Su dung OAuth 2.0 & OpenID Connect

SePay:
- Cong thanh toan tich hop trong ung dung
- Ho tro QR thanh toan, giao dich minh bang & an toan

Cloudinary:
- Luu tru hinh anh su kien, avatar, logo
- Tu dong tao responsive images

Render + Docker + GitHub Actions:
- Cloud hosting + CI/CD tu dong
- Docker dam bao nhat quan moi truong
